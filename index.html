Group Trip CardConcept prototype
Student case-competition prototype for MMT Young Turks Season 4 (Case 01, From Reel to Real Trip). It is not a MakeMyTrip product and is not connected to MakeMyTrip, WhatsApp or Instagram. No booking, hold or payment happens here. Prices are illustrative estimates, not live fares. Everything you enter stays in this browser; response codes are encoded, not encrypted, so the budget-privacy promise is a design rule the real product enforces on the server.
